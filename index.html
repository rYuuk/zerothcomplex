Blue Siege

Blue Siege is an idle tower defense game where you defend the structure against waves of aquatic invaders. Your automated defense system employs a variety of turrets with piercing bullets, laser beams, and explosive projectiles to fend off the enemy. Between waves, select powerful upgrades like multi-barrel weapons, energy shields, and emergency pulse attacks that clear the battlefield, then watch as your defenses handle increasingly challenging swarms.

Built using Unity and DOTS
