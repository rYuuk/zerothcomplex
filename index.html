IdleCore

Summary

IdleCore is a true idle base defence game. Defend the base against relentless waves of hostile aquatic creatures. Defeat each wave to earn currency, then invest them in powerful upgrades - add turrets, shield, and more, to strengthen your defenses and stay one step ahead of the aquatic threats. Defend the base on different types of planets. By defending successfully earn new skins of base and new style of turrets.


Features

  • Effortless Idle Combat: Automated turrets lock on and engage enemies, to enjoy the action hands free.
  • Smooth Experience: Dive into smooth gameplay backed by a clean, immersive UI and captivating visual design.
  • Explosive VFX: Feast your eyes on spectacular particle bursts that bring every turret blast and creature takedown to life.
  • Deep Upgrade System: Enhance the defenses with tiered improvements from reinforced armor to chain lightning, that scale with every victory.
  • Procedurally Unlocked Skill Trees: Discover randomized upgrade paths each run, ensuring fresh strategies and surprises every time you play.
  • Custom Mode: Play a custom wave by choosing upgrades and specifying the number of each enemy type.
